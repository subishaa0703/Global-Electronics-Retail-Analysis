# Global Electronics Retail Sales Analysis

# 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data from a global electronics retailer to identify trends in revenue, profitability, product performance, and geographical sales.

The goal is to transform raw sales data into actionable business insights using MySQL and Power BI.

# 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze sales and revenue trends over time.
- Identify top-performing product categories and products.
- Evaluate profitability and profit margins.
- Compare sales performance across countries.
- Understand changes in sales performance across different years.

# 🛠️ Tools Used

- MySQL: Data querying and SQL analysis.
- Power BI: Data visualization, dashboard development, and business reporting.
- CSV/Excel: Data storage and source files.

# 📊 Dashboard Overview

The interactive Power BI dashboard is organized into three analytical views:

# 1. Executive Overview
Provides a summary of business performance through key performance indicators and sales trends.

# 2. Product & Profitability
Examines product and category performance, revenue contribution, profit, and profitability trends.

# 3. Customer & Geography
Explores geographical sales performance and compares customer and revenue distribution across countries.

# 🔍 Key Business Insights

- Product performance: Computers contributed 38.1% of total revenue, making them the leading product category by revenue.
- Seasonal trends: December recorded the highest monthly revenue in each year from 2016 to 2019.
- Revenue trend: Revenue dropped significantly in 2020 compared with the preceding period.
- Geographical performance: The United States ranked first in sales, followed by the United Kingdom.
- Category performance: Home Appliances ranked second from 2017 to 2019 but moved to fourth place in 2020.
- Business performance: Revenue, quantity sold, and profit nearly doubled from 2017 to 2018, while the profit margin remained relatively stable.

# 💡 Conclusion

The analysis highlights differences in product performance, geographical contribution, and sales trends over time. These findings demonstrate how SQL-based analysis and Power BI visualizations can help businesses monitor performance and identify areas for further investigation.
